# Code of conduct

This project is a place to build a small tool well. Everyone taking part, in issues, pull requests, and discussions, is expected to follow these rules.

## Expected

- Be respectful, including when you disagree.
- Discuss the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change that is outside the project's scope.

## Not accepted

- harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- discriminatory language or behavior of any kind
- sharing someone's private information without their permission
- sexual language or imagery
- repeated disruption of a discussion after being asked to stop

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer, [@cris-wendler](https://github.com/cris-wendler) on GitHub. Reports are handled privately. The person reported will not be told who reported them.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block people from the project for behavior that breaks these rules. For a first minor problem the usual response is a private note. Serious or repeated problems lead to a block.

These rules apply in all project spaces and when someone represents the project elsewhere.
