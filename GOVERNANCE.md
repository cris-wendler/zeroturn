# Governance

ZeroTurn has one primary maintainer, listed in
[MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md), who merges pull requests and decides
the scope. Every decision is written down in
[docs/decisions.md](docs/decisions.md) with the evidence behind it. To
propose a change of scope, open an issue; if it is accepted it becomes an
entry in that file.

The reason for writing decisions down is that a small tool stays small
only when the refusals are as clear as the features.

As more people join they are added to MAINTAINERS.md, and this file
describes how decisions are shared.
